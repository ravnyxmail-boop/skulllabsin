Our commitment to service reliability and performance

1. Uptime Guarantee

Skull Hosting guarantees 99.9% network uptime for all VPS hosting services. This guarantee applies to the network connectivity between your VPS and the public Internet through our data center's border routers.

99.9% Uptime Guarantee

This translates to a maximum of:

  • 43.2 minutes of downtime per month
  • 8.76 hours of downtime per year

2. Service Credits

If we fail to meet our uptime guarantee, you may be eligible for service credits based on the following schedule:

Uptime Percentage Service Credit
99.0% - 99.89% 5% of monthly fee
95.0% - 98.99% 10% of monthly fee
90.0% - 94.99% 25% of monthly fee
Below 90.0% 50% of monthly fee

Credit Terms:

  • Credits are applied to the next billing cycle
  • Credits cannot be exchanged for cash
  • Maximum credit cannot exceed 100% of monthly service fee
  • Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident

3. Network Uptime

Network uptime is measured from our data center's border routers to the public Internet. We monitor network availability using multiple external monitoring points across different geographic locations.

Network Performance Standards:

  • Packet loss: < 0.1%
  • Latency: < 50ms to major US cities
  • Latency: < 100ms to European cities
  • Latency: < 150ms to Asian cities

4. Power Uptime

All our data centers maintain redundant power infrastructure with the following specifications:

  • Dual utility feeds from separate substations
  • N+1 or 2N UPS systems
  • On-site diesel generators with 48+ hours fuel capacity
  • Automatic transfer switches
  • Regular generator testing and maintenance

Power uptime guarantee: 99.99%

5. Support Response Times

We commit to the following support response times based on ticket priority:

Priority Level Description Response Time Update Frequency
Critical (P1) Service completely down < 15 minutes Every 30 minutes
High (P2) Severe degradation < 30 minutes Every 1 hour
Medium (P3) Partial impairment < 2 hours Every 4 hours
Low (P4) General inquiry < 4 hours Every 24 hours

6. Exclusions

The following situations are excluded from our SLA guarantee:

  • Scheduled Maintenance: Properly announced maintenance windows (minimum 72 hours notice)
  • Force Majeure: Events beyond our reasonable control including natural disasters, war, terrorism, riots, embargoes, acts of civil or military authorities, fire, floods, accidents, strikes, or shortages of transportation, facilities, fuel, energy, labor, or materials
  • Customer Actions: Problems caused by customer actions, including but not limited to misconfiguration, unauthorized modifications, or failure to follow best practices
  • Third-Party Services: Issues caused by third-party services outside our control (DNS providers, upstream networks, etc.)
  • DDoS Attacks: While we provide DDoS mitigation, sustained attacks that overwhelm our defenses may cause temporary service disruption
  • Customer Equipment: Problems related to customer-provided equipment or software

7. Claim Process

To request service credits for SLA violations:

  1. Submit a ticket through your customer portal within 30 days of the incident
  2. Include specific dates, times, and description of the outage
  3. Our support team will investigate and verify the claim
  4. Approved credits will be applied within 5 business days
  5. You will receive email notification of credit approval or denial

If you disagree with our determination, you may appeal the decision by contacting our escalation team at sla-appeals@skullhosting.com

8. Monitoring and Reporting

We continuously monitor our infrastructure and provide transparency through:

  • Public Status Page: Real-time status updates at status.skullhosting.com
  • Monthly Reports: Detailed uptime reports available in your customer portal
  • Incident Reports: Post-mortem analysis for significant incidents
  • API Access: Programmatic access to status information

Customers can also set up custom alerts for their services via SMS, email, or webhook notifications.
