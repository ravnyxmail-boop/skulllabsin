Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I deploy a VPS?

Your VPS is typically ready in under 60 seconds after order confirmation. You'll receive login credentials via email immediately.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Bitcoin, and bank transfers for annual plans.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your control panel. Changes take effect immediately.

Do you offer backups?

Yes, all plans include automated daily backups. You can also create manual snapshots anytime from your dashboard.

What's your uptime guarantee?

We guarantee 99.9% uptime for all our services. If we fall short, you're eligible for service credits per our SLA.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all VPS plans. No questions asked.
